# 10 — Bus Stops Pro

## Access model

The Pro demo is public, read-only and immediately explorable. It defaults to a useful England/area view with real public data or a conspicuously labelled dated snapshot if live national analytics are unavailable. Authentication gates only organisation-specific scope, saved settings, recipients, and email delivery. Do not expose personal or secret operational data.

## Control Tower

Above the fold: Network Health with confidence/coverage, active observed buses, punctuality, reliability, average delay, abnormal disruptions, source-health summary and area/time filters. Below: live operational map, priority exceptions, biggest delay burden, biggest abnormal disruptions, routes requiring attention, today’s outlook and deterministic intelligence summary.

Every tile drills into evidence and states definition, denominator, comparison window and freshness. Avoid KPI theater. If coverage is weak, lead with that fact.

## Live Operations

Map/list of current operational exceptions, vehicles and routes; filters by operator, area, route, severity, event and freshness. Present bunching, gaps, likely diversions, congestion and stale/missing feeds. Include lifecycle, evidence and recovery trend. Do not simulate dispatch controls.

## Routes and operators

Route comparison: punctuality, reliability, delay distribution, headway adherence, bunching/gaps, diversion evidence, worst corridors/time windows, baseline trend and weather sensitivity. Operator scorecards show raw and context-adjusted performance together. Suppress or caveat rankings for non-comparable coverage and service mix.

## Congestion

Hotspot map/table, road segment/corridor performance, direction and time, current versus typical, excess vehicle-minutes, affected routes/vehicles, occurrence frequency and confidence. Separate `Biggest delays` from `Most abnormal`. Overlay corroborating roadworks/incidents without claiming causation.

## Analytics

Delay-origin analysis, historical patterns, weather sensitivity, flood susceptibility, route risk, speed anomalies, data quality, and methodology. Charts offer tables/download of derived aggregates only, within licensing and quota constraints. No raw national GPS export.

## Disruptions and reports

Priority exception inbox with severity, abnormality, impact, confidence, source and status. Reports provide daily/weekly/monthly scoped derived summaries, accessible web/print views and bounded exports. Daily Brief browser view exactly matches the email snapshot’s facts.

## Settings

Organisation, permitted data scope, timezone, metric definition profile, regions/operators/routes, display preferences and Daily Brief settings. Public demo settings are ephemeral/local. Authenticated changes require authorization, validation and audit records.
