## Measurements and correctness

Run 41525648313 tested pruning against its unpruned control on one service date.
The five original profiling corridors agreed exactly. The twenty-query corpus
found one complete-result disagreement: Didsbury to Prestwich, Manchester.
Run 41526837062 added that corridor to the profiler and recorded both results.

The first recommended itinerary, arrival and two changes are identical. The
fewer-change alternative differs: pruned arrival 37,437 seconds, walking 790
seconds; unpruned arrival 37,434 seconds, walking 847 seconds. Both have one
change. This is not evidence that the primary journey was lost. It does fail
the new, stricter complete-result equivalence check. Do not suppress that
discrepancy or claim exact equivalence. The transfer frontier's dependence on
mutable labels warrants investigation before enabling pruning by default.
